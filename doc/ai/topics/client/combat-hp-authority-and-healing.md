- **HP authority:** `UpdateStats.hp` and legacy `GSV_SET_HPnMP` call `Reconcile_HP()` and update `m_iAuthoritativeHP`. HP increases apply immediately. Lower HP is folded into a later real damage presentation; it must not silently jump the visible bar.
- **Healing must be announced, or it does not exist.** `Reconcile_HP()` is the *only* thing that raises `m_iAuthoritativeHP`; a plain `Add_HP()` — client-side potion prediction (`CEnduranceItem::Proc`, `CUserDATA::Use_ITEM`), or any local heal — moves the visible bar and nothing else. `SetAuthoritativeHPFromDamageEvent` is deliberately **lower-only** (a queued `hp_after` is an absolute snapshot that may have been superseded while the presentation waited for its animation frame, and drain-on-death depends on out-of-order lowering being safe). So any server-side healing that ships **no** HP sync leaves the shadow HP stale, and the next damage checkpoint folds the bar right back to the pre-heal value. If you add a server heal path, give it a sync. The historical repro: potions restored HP silently and poison — which also suppresses the natural-regen sync — dragged the bar back once a second, so pots looked like they did nothing while poisoned even though server HP was correct the whole time.
- **Skill heals are announced by the effect packet itself (2026-09-20).** `gsv_EFFECT_OF_SKILL` now carries `m_iHP_AFTER` (moved up from `gsv_DAMAGE_OF_SKILL`, filled by every sender with the target's HP after `Skill_ApplyIngSTATUS`). For a direct heal — type 10/11 with a succeeded plain `AT_HP` slot, `CObjCHAR::IsDirectHealPayload` — `Recv_gsv_EFFECT_OF_SKILL` calls `ReceiveHealCheckpoint()` on the *target* at receive: the authority half of `Reconcile_HP` (shadow HP + supersession stamp, now factored out as `ReceiveAuthoritativeHP()`), with the visible raise **held**. The heal is then *presented* at the caster's action frame: the `AT_HP` branch of `ApplyEffectOfSkill` calls `RevealAuthoritativeHPRaise()`, which raises the bar to the shadow and never lowers it (the local `Get_SkillAdjustVALUE` + `Add_HP` remains only as the fallback for a payload with no checkpoint). Why receive-time and not action-frame for the shadow: a hit the server applied *after* the heal can reach its hit frame before the cast animation reaches its action frame, and it must reconcile against the healed shadow (its overshoot clamp then reveals the heal early, and the later reveal is a no-op). A hit applied *before* the heal but presented after it goes through the heal-in-flight guard — digit shown, bar floored at the healed HP — so "the next attack did no damage" after a heal is the honest rendering, not a bug. The old failure: the heal moved the visible bar only, the next hit's checkpoint sat above the stale shadow and the fold dragged the bar back to the pre-heal value, and only a regen sync (none at full HP) put it right — 613/984/1568 HP corrections in the 2026-09-15 log. Mirrored in `combat_presenter_tests` as `receive_heal_checkpoint` / `reveal_heal` (six cases). Log signature: `CombatTrace heal checkpoint received` at receive, `heal revealed` / `heal reveal no-op` at the action frame. Client and server ship together: the packet grew by four bytes.
- **The one exception is `StatusTick`.** `ApplyPresentedCombatDamage` passes `bAllowRaise = true` to `SetAuthoritativeHPFromDamageEvent` for `StatusTick` events only. They are produced solely by `Give_STATUS_DAMAGE` and presented synchronously in `recv_damage_event`, so there is no deferral window and the checkpoint cannot have been superseded — a DoT tick pins the bar to server truth in both directions. Every deferred and discard path must keep the default `false`. Poison damage is server-authored end to end; do **not** re-add a client-local poison subtraction in `CEndurancePack::Update` (it double-applied the tick and the fold clamped it back up).
- **A fresh checkpoint must also supersede, not just apply.** Being fresh is *not* on its own enough: `bStaleHealedCheckpoint` keys off `m_dwLastAuthoritativeSyncSeq`, so a checkpoint that does not advance that stamp leaves already-queued checkpoints looking valid, and the next one to reach its animation frame drags the bar back to its own pre-heal `hp_after`. `SetAuthoritativeHPFromDamageEvent` therefore advances `m_dwLastAuthoritativeSyncSeq` to `event.arrival_seq` (the event's own arrival order, not a fresh counter, so supersession stays exact regardless of when it is presented; never moves backwards) **whenever `bAllowRaise` is set — not only when the value actually rose.** Queued events do not touch the shadow until they present, so mid-deferral the shadow still holds *pre-hit* HP and a fresher checkpoint routinely reads as "lowering" against it while sitting well above the queued hit. Healing is detected checkpoint-against-checkpoint by the `m_iAuthoritativeHP > event.hp_after` clause at the older event's presentation — the only moment both values are known — and a later checkpoint above an earlier one can only mean the server healed in between. Gating the stamp on the shadow instead silently missed the common case.
- Both orderings are covered in `combat_presenter_tests`: **superseded** — shadow 600, swing queued at checkpoint 400, potion heals back to 600 with no sync, poison tick reports 550; the swing must reach its hit frame and leave the bar at 550 with its full digit. **Not superseded (control)** — monotonically falling checkpoints 400 then 380 prove no healing, so the swing must still fold to 380. Keep both: they are what separates "supersede everything older" from "supersede nothing".
- **Legacy skill HP checkpoints:** `GSV_DAMAGE_OF_SKILL` carries server-authored `m_iHP_AFTER`, and `ConvertDamageOfSkillToDamage()` copies that into the queued `DamageEvent.hp_after`. Do not derive legacy skill `hp_after` from current visible HP; `UpdateStats.hp` can arrive while the skill payload is still waiting on the caster action frame, and visible-HP synthesis double-applies reconciliation drift.
- **Heal-in-flight checkpoint staleness:** `hp_after` is an *absolute* checkpoint valid only when the server computed it. A heal/potion/regen sync can arrive on the same TCP game socket while a hit is still deferred to its animation/impact frame, leaving the queued checkpoint outdated (lower than current truth). `DamageEvent.arrival_seq` (client-local, stamped at packet *receive* via `CObjCHAR::NextHPAuthoritySeq()` — at `PushEffectedSkillToList` for deferred skills so the source-packet order survives, in `PushCombatDamageEvent` for immediate paths) is compared in `ApplyPresentedCombatDamage` against `m_dwLastAuthoritativeSyncSeq` (stamped in `Reconcile_HP`). When a later sync raised HP above the checkpoint (`m_dwLastAuthoritativeSyncSeq > event.arrival_seq && m_iAuthoritativeHP > event.hp_after`), the presenter honors the fresher authoritative HP instead of dropping to the stale checkpoint — preventing a bar dip-then-snap-up. The digit still shows the full `damage_value`. The guard never fires when no later sync exists (normal hits) or when the later sync is *lower* (more damage — handled by the existing checkpoint fold), so single-socket arrival order is the only ordering assumption. `arrival_seq` is not wire data; do not serialize it. The overshoot-clamp that raises the bar to the fresher HP is gated on `!has_pending_damage()`, so with another hit still queued (multi-hit skill, melee + skill) it is skipped and the bar would dip-then-snap; `ApplyPresentedCombatDamage` therefore *also* floors the visible bar at `min(visibleBefore, m_iAuthoritativeHP)` whenever the stale-healed guard fired, regardless of queue state (bounded by `visibleBefore` so a hit never visibly heals; lethal excluded). A residual ~0.1 s flash remains if the stale checkpoint presents a frame or two *before* the heal sync actually arrives — bounded, digit always correct, and a candidate for a future draw-layer eased/rolling HP bar (cosmetic only; must not touch authoritative HP/digits/death).
- **Digits vs HP:** `DamageEvent.damage_value` is the floating digit and feedback damage. Folded HP correction/checkpoint drift uses a local HP delta inside `ApplyPresentedCombatDamage()` and must not mutate `event.damage_value` or inflate `event.raw_damage`. Apply the displayed damage first, then clamp/fold only the remaining difference to the authoritative checkpoint; never add a pending correction blindly if the displayed hit already reached or passed `hp_after`.
